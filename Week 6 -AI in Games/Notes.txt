### Resuming Your Project After Restarting Your PC

* **1. Start Ollama (if not already running)**
  * Open the Start menu and launch the **Ollama** app, or open Command Prompt / PowerShell and run `ollama serve` (keep that terminal window open while testing).
  * Confirm it is running by visiting `http://localhost:11434` in your browser (it should say `Ollama is running`) or running `ollama list` in a terminal.

* **2. Verify Your Model Is Available**
  * Run `ollama list` in your terminal to confirm `llama3.2:3b` is listed.
  * If `llama3.2:3b` is missing, run `ollama pull llama3.2:3b` to download it again.

* **3. Reopen Your Unity Project**
  * Open **Unity Hub**, go to the **Projects** tab, and open your project (`Artificial Intelligence Integration`).
  * Confirm your scene (`SampleScene`), `SimpleOllamaNPC` script, and UI elements load properly.

* **4. Enter Play Mode and Test Dialogue**
  * Click **Play** at the top center of the Unity Editor.
  * Select an NPC personality from the dropdown, type a message into `InputField (TMP)`, and click **Send** (or press **Enter**).
  * Verify the reply appears in the `Scroll View` and scrolls cleanly without errors in the **Console**.

* **5. Check Your Saved CSV Telemetry File**
  * Open the Unity **Console** (`Ctrl + Shift + C`) after starting Play mode to find the `[SimpleOllamaNPC] Telemetry CSV path: ...` line.
  * Navigate to that folder (`Application.persistentDataPath`) in File Explorer to open or analyze your `.csv` telemetry logs.
