Narrative Systems Mini-Project

Estimated Time: 1–2 hours

Submission: GitHub

Task Brief

Create a text-based, narrative-driven story game with player choices using an online compiler:

  • OnlineGDB (C++)
  • Programiz (C#)

Your story should:

  • Include branching paths or outcomes (e.g., multiple endings).
  • Use a data structure such as a tree, graph, or linked list to manage story flow or choices.
  • Include input-driven decisions (typed choices, numbered options, etc.).
  • Print text clearly, with small pauses or formatting for readability.

You may base your story on original fiction or a mystery/survival scenario — be creative but concise.

Deliverables

  • A single source file (main.cpp or Program.cs) that runs correctly in the online compiler.

  • Clear code comments explaining your data structure and logic.

  • A short GIF or video showing your program running.

  • A 400-500 Words dev log that is a critical reflection on the task and learned lessons. This should include what you planned to do, how you did it, what changes/adaptations you had to make, and what valuable skills/knowledge did you gain.

  • Which data structure you used and why
  • How player choices are stored and navigated
  • What challenges you faced in implementing the narrative logic
